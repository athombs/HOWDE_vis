HOWD-E

Howdy!

Welcome — this site showcases HOWD‑E (High‑Order WENO Discretization – Explicit), a hypersonic CFD research code I developed during my PhD at Texas A&M.

The original goal was to enable high‑fidelity simulation of hypersonic flows over ablating vehicles under thermochemical nonequilibrium. That required a tool with capabilities that are difficult to find together in open-source CFD:

  • Very high‑order spatial discretization (6th+ for DNS‑class calculations)
  • Curvilinear multiblock structured grids (flight‑vehicle geometries)
  • Thermal and chemical nonequilibrium modeling
  • Massively parallel computation
  • Finite‑rate surface chemistry / surface ablation modeling
  • Algebraic, RANS, and LES turbulence modeling

This site is primarily a visualization portfolio and a quick overview of what the code can do. For usage details, theory, and setup, see the user manual (or contact me).
